Lead Management Tracker

A structured lead tracking system designed to organize prospects, monitor pipeline progress, manage follow-ups, and maintain accurate CRM records.

Project Overview

This lead management template demonstrates how prospects can be tracked from initial contact through qualification, follow-up, and conversion while keeping records organized inside a CRM environment.

Key Features

Lead Database

Store prospect information in one organized location.

Qualification

Categorize and prioritize leads based on status and quality.

Follow-up Schedule

Keep track of upcoming follow-ups and important dates.

Pipeline Progress

Monitor every stage of the sales process from prospect to customer.

Tools Used

HubSpot CRM Lead Management Pipeline Tracking LinkedIn Sales Navigator Google Sheets Reporting

Business Value

An organized lead tracker helps businesses reduce missed follow-ups, improve response times, maintain accurate customer records, and provide better visibility into the sales pipeline.

Note: This lead tracker is an original portfolio demonstration created to showcase CRM organization and lead management skills. No confidential client information is included.
